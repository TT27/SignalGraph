ORIGINAL OPEN-SOURCE EXAMPLE

SignalGraph Desk

A deterministic investigation workspace built as a self-contained Graphiti example. The offline engine compares analyst-labelled, structured claims across two periods, preserving exact source excerpts and separate observation and validity timestamps.

What the rules mean

A conflict is a pair of different single-valued assertions with overlapping validity windows. A revision has an explicit supersession link or non-overlapping windows. A removal requires explicit retraction. Missing later evidence stays unverified. Shared aliases remain ambiguous.

Scope and limits

The built-in demo is fictional; imported evidence retains its supplied source labels. Rules do not extract facts from arbitrary prose, judge source reliability, or establish truth. The optional Graphiti episode adapter is separate and externally unverified; it never replaces demo results silently.

Reproducible by design

The built-in synthetic dataset has 12 documents, 6 entities and 8 labelled detections. Imported investigations are separate and retain their supplied source labels. Temporal replay, reviews, watch rules and Markdown briefings operate locally.
